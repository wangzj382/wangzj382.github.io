About

Research should make difficult choices easier to understand.

I am completing a PhD at Fuzhou University, with expected graduation in June 2027. My research sits at the intersection of finance, macroeconomic policy and digital transformation.

I am especially interested in policy communication, financial technology, AI governance and economic resilience. I value research that is empirically careful, institutionally grounded and useful beyond the page.

02

Research themes

Four questions guide my work.

01Financial institutions and digital transformation

How do digital capabilities, artificial intelligence and data infrastructures change bank behaviour, risk management and financial intermediation?

02Policy communication and expectations

How does the language of central banks and public institutions shape market expectations, corporate decisions and macro-financial outcomes?

03AI governance and the Chinese context

What governance arrangements help organisations adopt AI productively while managing information, accountability and systemic risk?

04Economic growth and resilience

Which financial and policy mechanisms help firms and regional economies absorb shocks, adapt and return to sustainable growth?
